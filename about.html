About Us

This website is the Bahawalpur presence for Health Green World (HGW) products in Pakistan. It is built around the product catalogue published on greenworld.pk and the international HGW catalogue at hgwmundo.com.

What we do here

  • List genuine HGW product names and shop prices as checked on 26 October 2026.
  • Let visitors send a product order request or inquiry for branch verification.
  • Let visitors send an open career application (no vacancies are claimed).
  • Keep branch details in one config file so the owner can correct them in one place.

What this site does not claim

This site makes no medical claims about curing or treating any disease, publishes no reviews, certifications or branch street addresses that could not be verified from the sources above, and does not present a form submission as a confirmed order, delivery or job offer. Food supplements are not a substitute for professional medical advice — ask a qualified clinician about any health product.

Sources and price honesty

Prices in data/products.json are the greenworld.pk/shop listings checked on 26 Oct 2026. A small number of products show a different price on their individual product page; those cards are flagged “confirm before payment”. If a price changes on greenworld.pk, update data/products.json and the checked date in assets/js/config.js.
